Who We Are

A load of mates who let an AI build our games —
live, while we play them.

It's improv comedy where the game itself is the improv partner — Whose Line crossed with an AI dungeon master crossed with a GTA server. We shout an idea mid-match; minutes later the AI's built it. Sometimes bang-on, sometimes gloriously, unpredictably wrong — and that gap, between what we asked for and what we got, is the whole show. Nobody can script it. That's the point.

We're not gay — we just love our mates. We'll charge a horde back-to-back and mean it; we'll go down defending a pub we don't even own; and we'll absolutely leave you behind if the van's low on fuel. The tenderness is real. The cowboy hats are a bit.

What We Play

Three ways to bond over near-certain death.

Nothing's pinned down — the AI cooks up something new most weeks, and half of it we couldn't repeat if we tried. Here are three that stuck around long enough to earn a name:

Mode · Infected

28 Frags Later

A rage-virus rips through Los Santos and turns the nightlife feral. Back-to-back, one clip left, we hold the line. The AI keeps “improving” the horde — last week they learned to open doors. We don't talk about last week.

Co-op survival · escalating horde · questionable mercy
Mode · Pint

Pint of the Dead

Our Shaun-of-the-Dead tribute: barricade the boozer, ration the crisps, wait for it all to blow over. Rule one — don't lose the pub. Rule two — it's still your round.

Pub siege · hold-out defence · one (1) cricket bat
Mode · Chase

Scrap Run

Grab the loot, lose the law. A cops-and-robbers dash across the city with every siren in the state on your bumper. Someone always yells “split up!” — someone always shouldn't have.

Cops & robbers · heist chase · zero exit strategy
