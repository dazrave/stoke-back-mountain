Stokeback Mountain

Custom co-op game modes for GTA V, and here's the daft part — almost all of it is written live by an AI while we're mid-game. Someone shouts "the zombies should drag us out of the car", and a few minutes later the zombies drag us out of the car. Occasionally it does something else entirely. That's the fun.

What we play

🧟 28 Frags Later

Wave-based horde survival. One hit and you're gone. Shamblers shuffle, runners sprint, and one in twenty is a copper carrying spare ammo. They flank, jump off roofs, and haul you out of a slowing car.

🚐 The Campaign

A Shaun of the Dead-shaped road trip. The pub's dry, there's a boat at dawn, and twenty miles of infected in between. Fuel runs out, mates go down, and a burning plane occasionally lands on the road ahead.

🚓 Scrap Run

One fugitive on a dirt bike, everyone else is police. Line-of-sight GPS, 999 calls when you nick a car out of sight, a helicopter whose only job is keeping eyes on you. Tyres, not heads.

How it actually works

  1. 1 We play, and we shout ideas — out loud, or into the box below.
  2. 2 An AI reads them, works out what we were really after, and writes it up.
  3. 3 We give it a quick look, and it builds and deploys the change to the live server.
  4. 4 We're playing the new version minutes later. Sometimes it's exactly what we asked for.

Pitch an idea

A new mode, a twist on an old one, a mechanic, or just chaos. Every idea gets read, tidied up by the AI, and queued for the next build. The dafter the better — half the entertainment is watching how it gets interpreted.
